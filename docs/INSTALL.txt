StillDock 1.0.0

macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon and Intel

Drag StillDock to Applications, then open it from Applications.
Add photos with the + button or Command-O. Select JPEG/PNG and a size,
then choose a folder for the exported copies.

원본을 보존한 사진 공유용 복사본을 만듭니다.
StillDock을 Applications로 옮긴 후 실행하세요.
사진 추가 → 형식과 크기 선택 → 저장 위치 선택 순서로 사용합니다.

All processing stays on your Mac. Visible faces, addresses and text remain.
An output folder synchronized by another service follows that service's settings.
Output is 8-bit sRGB SDR; JPEG is lossy. Files may become larger.

Support and source: https://github.com/controlguy-ys/StillDock
